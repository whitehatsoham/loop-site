The problem

Ride apps are built for one-off trips. Most trips aren't one-offs.

For people who ride every day

Uber and Lyft get expensive fast when you need them five mornings a week. Surge pricing and per-trip fees make a daily commute unpredictable and hard to budget.

For the people driving them

Drivers wait for pings, chase surges, and never know what a week will pay. Income swings from day to day, and most of the fare goes to the platform.

Loop's answer

Match one rider with one driver for a set weekly schedule. The rider gets a reliable ride at a flat weekly rate. The driver gets guaranteed hours and a predictable paycheck. With fewer middleman costs, riders pay less and drivers keep more per ride.

How it works

Book a block, not a ride.

Set your schedule

Pick the days, the time window, and your pickup and drop-off. For example, home to office, 8 to 9am, Monday through Friday.

Get matched with a driver

Loop pairs you with one vetted driver whose route and hours fit yours. You meet once and keep the same driver going forward.

Ride every week

Your driver shows up at the same time each day. You pay one flat weekly rate. No surge, no re-booking, no stranger each morning.

Value

Cut out the per-trip middleman and both sides come out ahead.

Today's apps take a cut on every single ride. Loop prices a whole week at once, so there's less overhead to pass along.

Lower fares

Riders pay a flat weekly rate that is cheaper than booking the same trips one at a time.

Higher take-home

Drivers earn more per ride because less of the fare goes to platform fees and idle time.

Zero surprises

Both sides know the schedule and the price before the week starts.

Exact savings and earnings will be measured during the pilot. Numbers above are the goal, not a reported result.

For drivers

Consistent hours. Riders you already know.

A set weekly schedule

Fill your mornings with standing bookings instead of waiting for the next ping. Know your week's hours on Sunday night.

Predictable income

Weekly rates are agreed up front. Your pay doesn't depend on surge luck or how many trips the algorithm sends you.

Keep more of each fare

Loop's model has fewer per-trip costs, so more of what the rider pays ends up with you.

Build regulars

Drive the same commuters every day. Fewer no-shows, fewer awkward pickups, and a route you know cold.

What we've learned

People told us they'd switch if the price is right.

In our surveys, cost was the deciding factor. Riders said they would use a recurring-driver service if it came in cheaper than their current apps. So price is what the pilot is designed to prove.

Next step: a small pilot

A handful of drivers and weekday commuters, running for a few weeks. We'll track two numbers: what riders save versus their usual app, and what drivers earn per hour compared with driving on demand.
